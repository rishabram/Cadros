Summary:
Nancy Fields is requesting consideration of a Preliminary Plat for the property located at 4768 S
1175 W for approximately 0.72 acre. The applicant is proposing a two-lot subdivision. The
applicant received final plat approval in 2017 for this proposed subdivision but did not record the
approved plat within the approval timeframe.

The property is zoned R-1-10 with a minimum 10,000 square foot lot size. The proposed plat
intends to have two (2) lots varying in size from 10,263 square feet to 21,139 square feet. The
proposed subdivision has one lot with an existing single-family home on it and one proposed lot to
the south. To meet code requirements, the lot sizes must be at least 80’ wide. The minimum home
size required is 1,200 square feet for a one-story home or 1,875 for a two-story home. The setbacks
for the R-1-10 zone are as follows:

Front Yard                Side Yard                Side Yard Combined        Rear Yard
25 feet                   5 feet                   16 feet                   15 feet

As proposed the subdivision meets the set-back requirements for the R-1-10 zone.

A few modifications/additions will be required for Final Plat approval, these include the following
items including compliance with all applicable code requirements:
    • An amendment to the original subdivision plat.
    • Legal descriptions need to be correct and consistent throughout the plat.
    • A Stormwater Agreement is needed
    • A bond is needed for improvements in the right of way
    • Hydrants, street lights, and drive-ways need to be added to the site plan.
    • A Title Report less than 90 days old is submitted.
    • A stipulation needs to be added to the plat that parking is not allowed on the shared access
        easement.

Public Comment
As of June 9th, 2022 only one call has been received for this application. It was a request for more
information about the subdivision.
Analysis
General Compliance with Development Codes - Development Review
Committee
The proposed Preliminary Plat is compliant with the statutes in the Taylorsville Land Development
Code.

Findings of Fact
   1. This application was initiated by Nancy Fields.
   2. The property is zoned R-1-10.
   3. The applicant is requesting Preliminary Plat approval for a two (2) lot subdivision.
   4. The proposed plat meets the size and setback requirements for the R-1-10 zone.
   5. There is an existing single-family dwelling unit on the property that will remain intact, an
      additional single-family dwelling unit is being proposed on the south side of the property.
   6. This property was re-zoned from a R-1-15 to a R-1-10 by the Planning Commission on
      6/13/17 and the City Council on 7/5/17.

Staff Recommendation
Staff recommends that the Planning Commission approves the Fields Preliminary Plat for a two (2)
lot subdivision.

Conditions of Approval
   1.  The original plat for 4768 S 1175 W is amended.
   2.  The plat is recorded with Salt Lake County.
   3.  A Title Report is submitted and matches the property owner.
   4.  A Stormwater Agreement is needed
   5.  A bond is needed for improvements in the right of way
   6.  Hydrants, street lights, and drive-ways need to be added to the site plan.
   7.  Legal descriptions need to be correct and consistent throughout the plat.
   8.  A stipulation needs to be added to the plat that parking is not allowed on the shared access
       easement.
   9. The final plat is compliant with all applicable codes and ordinances.
   10. The applicant will comply with the requirements of all reviewing agencies.
   11. All applicable fees and bonding are paid prior to recording of the plat.

Recommended Motion
I move that we approve File #4S22/SUB-000226-2022 based on the Findings of Fact and
Conditions of Approval as stated in the Staff Report.
