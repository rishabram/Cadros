### 1. Opportunity Overview & Project Objective
The {{ISSUING_AGENCY: Salt Lake City Redevelopment Agency (CRA)}}, in partnership with the municipal planning division, invites proposals from qualified housing developers and community builders for the purchase and redevelopment of publicly owned surplus real estate. 

The objective of this solicitation is to deliver high-quality, price-restricted attainable infill housing. To de-risk private capital and accelerate project delivery, the City has completed advance site diagnostics and pre-approved an objective dimensional envelope. Discretionary legislative rezoning is not required.

### 4. Evaluation Criteria & Scoring Rubric
Proposals will be evaluated on a 100-point competitive scale emphasizing speed to completion and depth of affordability over speculative land purchase bids:

1. **Development Team Qualifications & Track Record (25 Points):** Demonstrated capability delivering missing-middle attached product on budget and on schedule; experience managing deed-restricted homeownership programs.
2. **Attainable Affordability & Pricing Guarantee (30 Points):** Contractual commitment to home sales pricing below the designated target price caps; structure of long-term owner-occupancy protections.
3. **Speed of Delivery & Timeline Certainty (25 Points):** Feasibility of schedule demonstrating building permit submission within 90 days of contract execution and commencement of vertical construction within 180 days.
4. **Site Design & Environmental Quality (10 Points):** Architectural compatibility, exterior material durability, private open space integration, and energy-efficient building systems.
5. **Financial Capacity & Land Offering Term (10 Points):** Equity commitment, lender interest letters, and alignment of proposed land consideration with the City's published residual range.

### 5. Submission Requirements
Respondents must submit one electronic PDF dossier organized as follows:
- **Tab A — Executive Summary & Team Profile:** Team composition, key personnel, Utah contractor licenses, and three comparable completed infill projects.
- **Tab B — Site & Architectural Concept:** Schematic site plan depicting the 16-unit configuration, standard floor plans, preliminary exterior elevations, and utility connection schematic.
- **Tab C — Development Pro Forma & Attainable Pricing Schedule:** Detailed development budget identifying hard construction, soft planning, and per-unit infrastructure costs, confirming sales prices at or below target caps.
- **Tab D — Proof of Financing:** Bank reference letters, proof of required equity funds, or pre-qualification letters from construction lenders.
- **Tab E — Community Benefit & Affordability Covenant Agreement:** Signed acceptance of the City's standard 30-year deed restriction and owner-occupancy enforcement mechanisms.
