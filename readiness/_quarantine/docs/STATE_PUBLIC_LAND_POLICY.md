# STATEWIDE PUBLIC LAND ATTAINABLE HOUSING READINESS FRAMEWORK
## An Upstream Technical Assistance Model Supporting Surplus Land Disposition Under House Bill 535

### Legislative Purpose & Structural Overview
Across the state, public entities hold surplus and underutilized real property that could accommodate attainable workforce housing. Under the state surplus land disposition framework of House Bill 535, public agencies are encouraged to identify surplus holdings and convert them into productive community assets. However, small municipalities and rural jurisdictions frequently lack the technical staff, planning budgets, and geometric tooling required to evaluate which parcels are truly buildable, which local zoning standards govern the site, and what development scale is economically viable.

This framework establishes a statewide pre-approval pathway that resolves entitlement and feasibility uncertainty before public land is offered for sale. By performing deterministic technical analysis upstream, the public sector removes speculative planning risk, enabling private and non-profit builders to compete directly on housing affordability and rapid delivery.

---

### Preservation of Municipal Land Use Authority
Participation in the public land readiness framework is entirely voluntary for cities and counties. Local governments maintain full constitutional and statutory authority over their zoning maps, comprehensive plans, and community standards. 

The framework does not preempt municipal prerogatives, alter established zoning classifications, or impose mandatory density mandates. Instead, the initiative offers local leaders transparent, parcel-specific evidence showing how their existing or reformed ordinances apply to their own land. A municipality may opt into the program for selected surplus parcels, retain discretionary review, or elect to adopt administrative by-right pathways for sites that satisfy its objective local criteria.

---

### State Risk Absorption & Program Funding
The primary barrier to activating surplus municipal land is upfront entitlement risk. Traditional development processes force prospective homebuilders to invest substantial private capital into architectural design, traffic impact studies, civil engineering, and legal fees before knowing whether a project will be approved. This dynamic inflates development carrying costs and filters out small, local builders.

Under this policy, the state absorbs this front-end risk:
- The state establishes a designated technical assistance grant fund that finances site-specific diagnostics, cadastral verification, and geometric yield modeling for participating local jurisdictions.
- By funding the analysis prior to disposition, the state eliminates speculative carrying costs for homebuilders.
- The state carries the entitlement risk by securing municipal consensus on objective dimensional standards and pre-approved building typologies before property conveyance.
- When surplus parcels are sold or ground-leased, the public sector delivers a shovel-ready entitlement envelope rather than raw, unvetted land.

---

### Non-Profit Public-Interest Technology Partnership
To ensure objective, reproducible analysis without expensive consultant overhead, the program partners with a non-profit decision-support laboratory. The non-profit partner provides the deterministic parcel screening engine, verified rule-store infrastructure, and automated packaging software at low, subsidized cost.

Key tenets of the non-profit operating model include:
- **Deterministic Independence:** Site feasibility and zoning compliance are calculated via verifiable software algorithms rather than subjective consulting narratives or ungrounded machine generation.
- **Open Standards & Data Integrity:** Schemas, evaluation rubrics, and source provenance are shared openly, enabling local planners to audit and reproduce all technical findings.
- **Cost Minimization:** Philanthropic and state technical assistance funding subsidizes the tool for capacity-constrained jurisdictions, ensuring that small town and rural planning departments receive the same analytical rigor as major metropolitan centers.

---

### Statewide Scale & Implementation Impact
The framework is designed for immediate statewide scalability across all counties and incorporated municipalities:
- **Unified Cadastral Pipeline:** A single standardized data schema ingests county assessor files, state geographic information layers, and local municipal zoning texts without requiring custom software engineering for each jurisdiction.
- **Standardized Implementation Toolkits:** Every analyzed parcel yields an administrative readiness packet comprising an objective compliance checklist, pre-approved building envelopes, and a turnkey starter solicitation ready for immediate public advertisement.
- **Expanded Builder Universe:** By providing clear dimensional parameters and pre-cleared entitlement tracks, the program invites small, local, and emerging general contractors who cannot otherwise afford the risk of prolonged discretionary approvals.

Through this coordinated partnership—combining voluntary municipal authority, state risk absorption, non-profit technical infrastructure, and statewide scale—surplus public land moves swiftly from dormant municipal balance sheets into attainable homeownership for the state workforce.
